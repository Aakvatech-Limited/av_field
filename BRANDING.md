AV Field is open-source software. Forks may use independent branding. Legal attribution is distinct from trademark endorsement.

The official application's About / Legal Notices view identifies the original developer, copyright holder, AGPLv3 license, additional terms, and source repository. Its legal text is bundled for offline use.

Suggested attribution:

Distributors may present applicable reasonable attribution in an appropriate legal-notices screen or in supporting documentation, as applicable. No permanent Aakvatech logo, fixed footer, or splash screen is imposed.
